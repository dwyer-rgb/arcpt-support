Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

arc PT does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All information you enter — exercises, programs, photos, videos, notes, and measurements — is stored only on your device. We have no servers and no accounts, and we do not use analytics or tracking.

Media you add stays on your device. Video links you add open in your browser. Sharing a program uses your device's standard share sheet and is initiated by you.

Because no data leaves your device, we have no access to it. Deleting the app removes its data from your device.

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at arcpt.support@gmail.com.
