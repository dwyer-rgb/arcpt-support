Need help with arc PT?

arc PT is a physical-therapy companion — a calm, focused way to do your prescribed rehab and track your progress, and a toolkit for therapists to build and share client programs.

For questions, bug reports, feedback, or anything else, email us and we'll get back to you:

arcpt.support@gmail.com

Common questions

Where is my data stored? Everything you enter stays on your device. arc PT has no account and no server. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I share a program with my client? In therapist mode, open the client's program and use Share to send it as a file or a text summary from your phone.

I deleted the app — is my data gone? Yes. Because data lives only on your device, removing the app removes its data. Export a program first if you want to keep it.
